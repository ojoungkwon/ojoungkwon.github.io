## Research Interests

Graph theory, combinatorial optimization, structural graph theory, and parameterized complexity.

Tree-width · Rank-width · Mim-width · Vertex-minors · Minors · Erdős–Pósa · Graph coloring · χ-boundedness · Dense graph classes · Induced subdivisions · Polynomial kernelization · Holes · Intersection graphs
