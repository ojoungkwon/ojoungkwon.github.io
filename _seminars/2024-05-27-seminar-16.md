Date : May 27 (2:30pm)

Speaker : Dong Yeap Kang (IBS Ecopro)

Title: Random graphs/hypergraphs and thresholds

Abstract:

Random graphs is one of the most studied objects in probabilistic combinatorics. The study of random graphs was initiated by Erdos and Renyi in 1960s, where they observed a fundamental phenomenon called thresholds or phase transitions in random graphs. In this lecture, we will study the thresholds for some basic properties in random graphs/hypergraphs as well as the recent development by Frankston, Kahn, Narayanan, and Park on the Kahn--Kalai conjecture.
