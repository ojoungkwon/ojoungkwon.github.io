Title : Hitting times for Hamilton cycles in random sparsifications

Speaker : Seonghyuk Im (KIAS)

Date : Apr 27, 2026 (11:00)

Place : Natural Science Building 702, Hanyang University

Abstract : Abstract: Consider the random subgraph process on a base graph $G$ with $n$ vertices, where we generate a sequence $\\{G\_t\\}\_{t=0}^{\|E(G)\|}$ by adding the edges of $G$ one by one in a uniformly random order to an empty graph. A foundational theorem of Bollobás, and independently Ajtai, Komlós, and Szemerédi, establishes that when the base graph is $K\_n$, the hitting time for a Hamilton cycle coincides exactly with the hitting time for minimum degree $2$ with high probability.

In this talk, we explore two distinct generalizations of this problem. First, in the setting of pseudo-random graphs, we show that there exists a constant $C &gt; 0$ such that for any $(n,d,\\lambda)$-graph $G$ satisfying $d/\\lambda \\ge C$, the hitting time for Hamiltonicity coincides with the hitting time for minimum degree $2$ with high probability. This resolves long-standing questions by Frieze--Krivelevich (2002) and Alon--Krivelevich (2019).

Second, we extend this hitting time phenomenon to the hypergraph setting via Dirac-type conditions. We prove that if $G$ is an $r$-uniform hypergraph with minimum degree $\\delta(G) \\geq (1/2^{r-1}+\\varepsilon)\\binom{n-1}{r-1}$, the hitting time for a Berge Hamilton cycle matches the hitting time for minimum degree $2$ with high probability, successfully extending Johansson's result to hypergraphs.

This talk is based on joint work with Minseo Kim, and joint work with Yaobin Chen, Yu Chen, and Yiting Wang.
