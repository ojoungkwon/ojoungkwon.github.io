Title : Odd coloring and strong odd coloring on planar graphs

Date : Nov 27, 2024 (3:00pm)

Place : Natural Science Building 744, Hanyang University

Speaker : Hyemin Kwon (권혜민 박사, KIAS)

Abstract : Recently, odd coloring and strong odd coloring has been introduced, which is a relaxation of square coloring. An odd coloring of a graph $G$ is a proper coloring of $G$ such that for each non-isolated vertex $v$, there is a color appearing an odd number of times in $N\_G(v)$. A strong odd coloring of a graph $G$ is a proper coloring of $G$ such that for each vertex $v$, each color appearing in $N\_G(v)$ appears an odd number of times. In this talk, we introduce results of odd coloring and strong odd coloring on planar graphs.
