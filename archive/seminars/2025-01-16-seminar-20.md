<h3>Abstract</h3>

This talk introduces the graph parameter twin-width from the lesser known point of view of permutations.

I will first present basic notions of combinatorics on permutations, and a fundamental result: the Marcus-Tardos theorem

which bounds the density of matrices avoiding a given pattern. Using this theorem, we will see how Guillemot and Marx

invented the definition and main techniques for what is now known as twin-width, to design an algorithm which efficiently

finds patterns in permutations.

I will conclude by presenting a recent decomposition result: permutation with bounded twin-width are build from a bounded product of permutations of twin-width 0.

This is joint work with Édouard Bonnet, Romain Bourneuf, and Stéphan Thomassé.
