<h3>Abstract</h3>

Recently, odd coloring and strong odd coloring has been introduced, which is a relaxation of square coloring. An odd coloring of a graph $G$ is a proper coloring of $G$ such that for each non-isolated vertex $v$, there is a color appearing an odd number of times in $N\_G(v)$. A strong odd coloring of a graph $G$ is a proper coloring of $G$ such that for each vertex $v$, each color appearing in $N\_G(v)$ appears an odd number of times. In this talk, we introduce results of odd coloring and strong odd coloring on planar graphs.
