Lecture Series on Combinatorics at Hanyang University

Lecture series on Combinatorics at Hanyang University will take place from Aug 1 to Aug 2 at Hanyang University in Seoul, Korea.

The aim of this lecture is to cover basic theory on infinite graphs.

Registration : [https://forms.gle/AHkUzUpZPJ2RmPpv6](https://forms.gle/AHkUzUpZPJ2RmPpv6)

11:00am - 12:30am : Lecture 1. Infinite Graphs: Compactness, Transfinite Induction, and Other Basic Methods

12:30am - 2:30pm : Lunch

2:30pm - 4:00pm : Lecture 2. Paths, Trees, and Ends in Infinite Graphs

8\.2.

11:00am - 12:30am : Lecture 3. Locally Finite Graphs with Ends: An Introduction to Topological Infinite Graph Theory

12:30am - 2:30pm : Lunch
