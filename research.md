Structural and algorithmic questions in graph theory.

## Graph structure and width

I study how graph width parameters capture structural complexity, with a particular interest in tree-width, rank-width, mim-width, and related notions of depth. My work explores structural characterizations and decomposition methods, and how these can be used to design algorithms for sparse and dense graph classes.

*Selected papers*

- [Reduced bandwidth: a qualitative strengthening of twin-width in minor-closed classes (and beyond)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.11858) — JCTB, 2026.
- [Branch-depth: Generalizing tree-depth of graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.11988) — European Journal of Combinatorics, 2020.
- [Mim-width I. Induced path problems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.04536) — Discrete Applied Mathematics, 2020.

## Graph containment

Which structures must appear in a graph when its complexity is sufficiently large? I investigate this question through graph containment relations, especially vertex-minors and pivot-minors, and study how excluding particular structures constrains a graph.

*Selected papers*

- [The grid theorem for vertex-minors](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.08113) — JCTB, 2023.
- [Tree pivot-minors and linear rank-width](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.00561) — SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics, 2021.
- [Packing and covering induced subdivisions](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.07581) — SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics, 2021.

## Packing and covering

I study when a graph must contain either many disjoint structures of a prescribed type or a small vertex set meeting all such structures. These Erdős–Pósa questions connect cycles, paths, and graph containment, with a particular focus on induced structures and group-labelled graphs.

*Selected papers*

- [Erdős-Pósa property of A-paths in unoriented group-labelled graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.05372) — Combinatorica, 2026.
- [A unified Erdős-Pósa theorem for cycles in graphs labelled by multiple abelian groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.09488) — Mathematische Annalen, 2025.
- [Erdős-Pósa property of chordless cycles and its applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.00667) — JCTB, 2020.

## Coloring and intersection graphs

I investigate how structural restrictions on graphs influence their chromatic number, especially through χ-boundedness and excluded vertex-minors. I also study intersection graphs and digraphs, exploring how their representations lead to useful structural and algorithmic properties.

*Selected papers*

- [Classes of intersection digraphs with good algorithmic properties](https://doi.org/10.1002/jgt.23065) — Journal of Graph Theory, 2024.
- [Classes of graphs with no long cycle as a vertex-minor are polynomially chi-bounded](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.04278) — JCTB, 2020.
- [Chi-boundedness of graph classes excluding wheel vertex-minors](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.07851) — JCTB, 2019.

## Parameterized algorithms

I develop algorithms that exploit structural parameters to solve computationally difficult graph problems. My interests include fixed-parameter algorithms and polynomial kernelization, particularly for vertex deletion problems and for covering and packing problems on restricted graph classes.

*Selected papers*

- [Unified almost linear kernels for generalized covering and packing problems on nowhere dense classes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.06660) — Journal of Computer and System Sciences, 2026.
- [A polynomial kernel for 3-leaf power deletion](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.04249) — Algorithmica, 2023.
- [A polynomial kernel for Distance-Hereditary Vertex Deletion](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.07229) — Algorithmica, 2021.
